My personal commit message rule:
Every commit message I write must answer "what changed and why" in one clear sentence. I use the imperative mood (add, fix, update) and keep it under 72 characters. I never use vague words like "stuff", "fix", or "changes" on their own. If the change needs more context, I add a short body below the subject line.
